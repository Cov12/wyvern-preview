Who we are

Metrology systems that deliver results

Wyvern is a specialist supplier of industrial measurement and positioning systems to the Advanced Engineering sector.

We provide measurement systems for cleanliness testing, the inspection of circular and angular geometry along with systems for metrology integration. We also provide training, service, repair, and calibration services for these systems.

Product areas

Cleanliness Testing

Technical cleanliness is a critical element of manufacturing quality and process control — move beyond final inspection toward proactive contamination prevention.

CMM & Metrology

Additional axes for metrology integration — CMMs, measurement arms and laser trackers.

Positioning & Indexing

Precision shop floor and laboratory positioning and indexing.

Circular Geometry

Precision shop floor circular geometry inspection systems — runout, roundness, eccentricity, circular flatness and parallelism.
